Key Features

Inventory Tracking

Easily add, update, search, and remove inventory items with minimum quantity tracking.

Stock & Expiry Alerts

Receive automated alerts for low stock, items nearing expiration, and expired items, both in the table and a dedicated section.

Usage Reports

Monitor stock usage over time to identify trends and optimize ordering.

Browser Local Storage

The inventory data is saved locally in browser via LocalStorage.
